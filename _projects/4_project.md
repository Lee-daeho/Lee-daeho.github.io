We made LED-POV leveraging C#, Micro Controller. When a user upload an image to C# program, it transform the image into points to match the coordinate of LEDs. Then we make the motor spin to show the POV.

I was a team member and sub software & circuit developer. I worked with 4 teammates, two of them desgined and manufactured the LED-POV and the other two developed software & circuit system. My job was deploying desgining and deploying circuit system, building bluetooth communication system between C# (PC) <-> MicroController.
