"Please note that if you don't wear a face mask, you restrict to ()" with Youjin Choi, Sooyeon Ahn, JuChan Seo will be exhibited during 2021. 03. 31 ~ 2021. 04. 18 in Seoul Art Center.

We made "Please note that if you don't wear a face mask, you restrict to ()" leveraging python, pytorch, and generative AI. During the COVID-19, people wore masks for health reasons. We developed a model to generate the occluded parts of a person's face that are hidden by a mask.

I was a team member and main software developer. I worked with 3 teammates, two of them designed concept and idea and the other one help me as a sub software developer. My job was developing model that generates occluded parts of a person.

We won the New Media Art Contest from Seoul Art Center and Korea Electric Power Corporation.
