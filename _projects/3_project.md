We made 3-DOF Delta Robot leveraging C#, Micro Controller, and Inverse Kinematics. When a user draw something in C# program, it calculates the position of the pen to make the robot draw. Then, through the inverse kinematics, the system calculates angles of three servo motors.

I was a team leader and main software & circuit developer. I worked with 3 teammates, two of them desgined and manufactured the robot and the other one help me as a sub software & circuit developer. My job was deploying inverse kinematics, desgining and deploying circuit system, building bluetooth communication system between C# (PC) <-> MicroController.

Through the project, we had been rewarded four Dean's Diessertion Awards (ADBL 수행 우수 장려상, 교과목 과제수행 우수, ADBL 인기상).
